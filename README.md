# SAA-C03 Field Guide

> 🚧 **Under construction, launching November 2026.** Things will move, break and change until then.

An interactive, open-source study guide for the **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03)** exam, built in public while studying for it.

| Part | What you get |
|---|---|
| 📖 **Guide** | Organised by the official exam guide's 4 domains and task statements: the idea, comparison tables, "if the exam says… → think…" cues and common traps, linked to AWS documentation |
| 🎬 **Animated diagrams** | Step through how a request actually flows (S3 + KMS, ALB + Auto Scaling, VPC endpoints…), toggle options and break things on purpose |
| 🧪 **Terraform labs** | Deploy what you just watched on your own account, with cost estimates, tests and destroy instructions |
| ✅ **Practice tests** | Free, exam-style questions written from scratch and reviewed by a human, with links back to the guide, diagram and lab for each one |

Everything is cross-linked: get a question wrong and the review screen sends you to the guide section, the animated diagram and the lab for that concept.

## Status

Early development. The repository layout, tooling and first diagrams are being built now; content follows the exam domains. Watch or star the repo to follow along.
